5. Masalalarning to’liq yechimi

1-variant

C1. Berilgan: q = 36 C; t = 2 min = 120 s; A = 108 J.
(a) I = q / t = 36 / 120 = 0,3 A.
(b) U = A / q = 108 / 36 = 3 V.
Javob: I = 0,3 A; U = 3 V.
C2. Berilgan: R = 12 Ω; U = 6 V.
(a) I = U / R = 6 / 12 = 0,5 A.
(b) U = I·R = 0,3·12 = 3,6 V.
Javob: I = 0,5 A; U = 3,6 V.
C3. Berilgan: R₁ = 6 Ω; R₂ = 12 Ω; U = 18 V.
(a) Ketma-ket: R = R₁ + R₂ = 18 Ω; I = U / R = 18 / 18 = 1 A.
(b) Parallel: R′ = R₁·R₂ / (R₁ + R₂) = 72 / 18 = 4 Ω; I′ = U / R′ = 18 / 4 = 4,5 A. I′ / I = 4,5 / 1 = 4,5.
Javob: (a) R = 18 Ω, I = 1 A; (b) tok kuchi 4,5 marta ortadi.
D. (a) Parallel: C = C₁ + C₂ = 2 + 3 = 5 µF.
(b) Ketma-ket: 1/C = 1/C₁ + 1/C₂ = 1/2 + 1/3 = 5/6, C = 6/5 = 1,2 µF.
Javob: parallel 5 µF; ketma-ket 1,2 µF. Ketma-ket ulanganda umumiy sig’im har bir kondensator sig’imidan kichik, parallel ulanganda esa ulardan katta (5 / 1,2 ≈ 4,2 marta).

2-variant

C1. q = 150 C; t = 5 min = 300 s; A = 900 J. (a) I = 150 / 300 = 0,5 A. (b) U = 900 / 150 = 6 V.
Javob: I = 0,5 A; U = 6 V.
C2. R = 15 Ω; U = 9 V. (a) I = 9 / 15 = 0,6 A. (b) U = 0,4·15 = 6 V.
Javob: I = 0,6 A; U = 6 V.
C3. R₁ = R₂ = 10 Ω; U = 20 V. (a) R = 10 + 10 = 20 Ω; I = 20 / 20 = 1 A.
(b) R′ = 10·10 / 20 = 5 Ω; I′ = 20 / 5 = 4 A; I′ / I = 4.
Javob: (a) R = 20 Ω, I = 1 A; (b) tok kuchi 4 marta ortadi.
D. (a) C = 4 + 12 = 16 µF.
(b) 1/C = 1/4 + 1/12 = 4/12 = 1/3, C = 3 µF.
Javob: parallel 16 µF; ketma-ket 3 µF. Ketma-ket ulanganda umumiy sig’im eng kichik kondensator sig’imidan ham kichik, parallel ulanganda esa ularning yig’indisiga teng (16 / 3 ≈ 5,3 marta katta).

  • Qamrov: 9–29-§ larning har biridan kamida bittadan topshiriq bor.
  • Hisoblar tekshirilgan: C1 (0,3 A, 3 V; 0,5 A, 6 V), C2 (0,5 A, 3,6 V; 0,6 A, 6 V), C3 (18 Ω, 1 A, 4 Ω, 4,5 marta; 20 Ω, 1 A, 5 Ω, 4 marta), D (5 µF, 1,2 µF; 16 µF, 3 µF).
